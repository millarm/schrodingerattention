# Next-level productive-diversity plan review

## Verdict: CHANGES REQUIRED

The proposed grid-route task is a credible minimal test of learned, verified, multi-solution behavior. Its exact completion-count teacher, independent shortest-path verifier, composition split, full-support novelty idea, active eight-scalar softmax control, common-random-number decoding, and separation of usable learning, architecture advantage, and mechanistic dependence are strong. ## Required corrections

1. **Make the novelty exclusion set equal to all supervised route support.** The training targets supervise every state on every training shortest-path DAG. Therefore the exclusion inventory must contain the canonical signature of every shortest suffix route from every such supervised `(map, current, goal)` state, not only complete routes from each original problem start. State explicitly how action sequences transform under each of the eight square symmetries, whether route reversal is or is not an equivalence, and use one deterministic canonical byte representation. Hash the complete exclusion set and independently verify membership/canonicalization. Retain the existing `M_novel >= 4` and 256/512 feasibility gate unchanged; failure is an honest domain-feasibility result.

2. **Freeze the policy used for proper scores and its state-bank weighting.** Cross-entropy, Brier, `KL(q||p)`, entropy, and nonoptimal mass must use the same locally legal masked-and-renormalized action distribution used by rollout—never an optimal-action mask—and define behavior if numerical legal mass is zero. Build the test bank before training from all deduplicated goal-reachable nonterminal `(map,current,goal)` states in the reserved problems, and state the exact equal-state (then map/seed) aggregation. Report illegal mass from the unmasked head separately if desired; do not mix it into the masked-policy proper score. This prevents ambiguity about whether the learning claim measures the deployed sampler or raw logits.

3. **Bind quality matching to the primary novelty stratum and strengthen pilot headroom.** Select temperature using validation only as proposed, but apply the >=88%, <=1 pp paired quality eligibility and the one-sided noninferiority test on the frozen `M_novel >= 4` IL stratum that supplies the primary coverage effect. Continue to report all 512 IL problems separately. The softmax pilot must successfully attain the proposed 90% validation quality target within tolerance (or the declared deterministic fallback must satisfy the match) before its diversity-headroom check is meaningful; 80% greedy completion and 60% T=1 validity alone do not establish a usable quality-matched operating point. Stop/re-plan if that operating point or headroom is unavailable.

4. **Define the primary aggregation and uncertainty calculation exactly.** Specify that per-problem coverage is first averaged within each canonical obstacle map, then maps are averaged within each seed, and the paired architecture effect is computed from the five seed means (or freeze another explicit hierarchy). Give the exact two-sided seed-level t-interval and one-sided noninferiority formulas/degrees of freedom; define the map-cluster bootstrap resampling unit, number of replicates, RNG seed, and percentile/BCa rule. State whether the >=88%, <=1 pp, >=0.05, >=10% relative, and ID-regression gates apply to the equal-seed aggregate, each seed, or both. Define relative gain when softmax coverage is zero. These choices cannot be deferred to reporting.

5. **Fully specify secondary diversity and paired sampling quantities.** Define `pass@32` (at least one verified shortest route among all 32 attempts), the concentration statistic and its treatment when `V=0`, and every ratio's zero-denominator convention. Freeze cardinal-action order for inverse-CDF sampling, rollout/sample RNG construction, and the mapping of common uniform variates across architectures/dt0. Confirm K=8/16 are prefixes and secondary only. This keeps temperature and entropy controls from acquiring implementation-dependent degrees of freedom.

6. **Define the dt0 estimand and budget gate before execution.** Let the primary paired gain and same-weight dt0 gain be explicit quantities and give the exact removal fraction, denominator, sign/zero convention, aggregation order, and four-of-five rule. Clarify that a dt0 reduction can show inference-time dependence on evolution; persistence under dt0 can still be compatible with training-mediated effects and does not invalidate usable learning. Before paired results, freeze a conservative operation-level time projection for five paired trainings, all checkpoints/controls, cached logits, K rollouts, bootstrap/audit, failed attempts, and reserve under one cumulative hard elapsed cap. Specify lock/deadline behavior and require the feasibility profile plus remaining charged budget to fit that projection; otherwise stop before comparison.

## Accepted elements to preserve

- One finite 6x6 route task, exact BFS/DAG completion counts, and `q(a|s)=C(next)/C(s)` teach a uniform distribution over complete optimal continuations rather than a single arbitrary reference.
- II/LL training and unseen-map ID evaluation versus IL composition testing, map-level split ownership, eight-symmetry map canonicalization, fixed generation seeds, distance/`log2(M)` matching, and pretraining feasibility inventory provide a defensible held-out design.
- The rollout verifier counts only independently verified shortest completions, never masks nonoptimal moves, and charges invalid, duplicate, and overlong samples.
- The main comparison is against an active capacity-matched softmax control; ordinary softmax remains pilot-only. Matching base initialization, batches, exposure budgets, and optimizer settings while acknowledging unequal initial functions is fair.
- Full quality–diversity curves, validation-only temperature selection, entropy matching as secondary, raw yield alongside oracle-normalized coverage, five paired seeds, and random/initial controls address the principal temperature, luck, and ambiguity confounds.
- The usable-learning gate correctly prevents a failed benchmark from becoming evidence against productive diversity, and the language avoids claims of creativity, quantum randomness, calibration, or general architectural efficacy.
