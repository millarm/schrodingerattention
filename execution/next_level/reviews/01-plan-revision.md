The pretraining bank is fixed from deduplicated goal-reachable nonterminal states; state, map, and seed weighting are declared. Gate scope and the zero-softmax-coverage relative-gain convention are explicit.

Raw useful yield remains alongside oracle-normalized coverage.

A conservative 1.5x operation projection plus 300-second reserve, prior-charge inclusion, 7,200-second cap, failed-attempt charging, interrupting deadline, immutable outputs, lock discipline, and explicit process exit are required before comparison.

## Fairness and evidential assessment

- The teacher `q(a|s)=C(next)/C(s)` is the correct local distribution for uniform sampling over complete shortest suffixes; it avoids a single-reference target and avoids incorrectly choosing uniformly among optimal actions.
- II/LL training and unseen-map ID evaluation versus IL composition testing, map-level split ownership under symmetry canonicalization, fixed seeds, and distance/solution-count matching isolate the intended compositional shift while preventing map and route leakage.
- The eight learned score/value scalars make the main softmax control active and parameter-count matched. Removing the ordinary-softmax training arm keeps the primary design minimal; initial and random controls remain sufficient for the usable-learning gate.
- Validation-only temperature matching, full quality-diversity curves, entropy matching as secondary, common random numbers, exact rollout verification, invalid-sample charging, and no oracle reranking prevent temperature or invalid uniqueness from masquerading as productive diversity.
- Five paired seeds are appropriately treated as the architecture replication unit; map bootstraps are complementary and are not promoted to independent model replications. Wide intervals remain explicitly inconclusive.
- The thresholds are demanding but symmetric and predeclared. A failed feasibility, pilot, quality-match, learning, precision, or budget gate cannot be reinterpreted as evidence against creativity or in favor of the architecture.
