## Question and scope

The previous screen found little accuracy change when evolution was disabled.
Determine whether (a) attention probabilities barely change, or (b) appreciable
attention changes have little downstream effect. This is retrospective analysis
of retained weights, not a new training experiment or proof of useful interference.
The original duplicate-attempt provenance and invalid timing comparison remain
limitations. Do not modify or overwrite any prior source, checkpoint, dataset,
report, or output. No training, tuning, parameter sweep, or paid resources.

Use retained Schrödinger checkpoints at step 2000 for seeds 11, 22, 33, each
with its own stored `evaluation.npz`. Analyze all saved conditions: validation
seen/d4, test seen/d4, heldout/d4, seen/d8, heldout/d8. Stratify XOR and COPY.
The stored arrays are 512 validation examples and 1024 per test condition.
Hash and verify checkpoints, arrays, metadata, original implementation,
configuration, contract and this plan before computation. No regenerated data.
Independently trained softmax checkpoints are not needed for the primary
question; omitting them avoids conflating intervention and training differences.

## Two separate comparisons

1. **Direct local effect at fixed input.** Trace the normal evolved model.
   At each layer's identical normalized hidden states, use its actual Q/K/V
   projections and learned gamma/dt. Compare evolved A with softmax(S). Neither
   input states nor values may change between these two local calculations.
   This is the primary attention comparison; at layer 2 its input has already
   been generated by the normal evolved earlier layer.
2. **Full-path intervention.** Trace the same weights with effective dt=0 in
   every layer and compare with the normal full path. Layer-2 inputs may now
   differ. Report propagated hidden-state and attention changes separately;
   never describe them as a fixed-input local effect. Final outputs come from
   these full paths, and must reproduce the original model API.

## Recorded measurements

Store row-level arrays (not only averages), retaining sample index, operation,
label, condition, seed, layer, head, and query position. Compressed NPZ is fine.

- Direct row total variation: TV = 0.5 * sum_key(abs(A_evolved-softmax(S))).
  Report mean, median, p90, p95, p99, maximum, fractions >=0.01 and >=0.05.
  Stratify per seed/condition/operation/layer/head, with all query rows and
  CLS query row 0 separately. TV is probability mass redistributed, bounded
  approximately [0,1]; no signed cancellation. Also retain maximum keywise
  absolute change per row.
- Direct head value-output change Y=A@V: per-row absolute RMS and relative
  RMS against Y_softmax (denominator max(reference RMS,1e-8)); record reference
  magnitude so near-zero denominators are visible. After concatenation and
  learned output projection, record the same absolute/reference/relative RMS
  per row, all rows and CLS separately. Output projection bias is included
  consistently in both outputs; its cancellation in differences is natural.
- Full-path attention TV and post-layer hidden-state RMS differences are
  labelled propagated. Retain layer-2 normalized-input RMS difference to prove
  the distinction; layer-1 normalized inputs should agree numerically.
- Final normal and dt0 logits/probabilities, per-example cross-entropy,
  prediction, correctness, and disagreement. Report mean/p95/max absolute
  change in p(class1); absolute change in binary logit margin; signed and
  absolute per-example loss change. Define loss benefit as CE_dt0-CE_normal:
  positive means evolution helps. Report normal/dt0 accuracy and signed delta,
  disagreement rate, mean benefit and sample variability. Keep XOR/COPY and
  each condition/seed separate. Mean signed loss alone is insufficient.
- Effective evolution scale per example/layer/head: operator norm of dt*H
  from eigvalsh(H), plus RMS eigenphase = abs(dt)*||H||_F/sqrt(sequence length).
  Gamma/dt alone do not determine the intervention size. Phase dispersion per
  query uses probability-weighted circular dispersion
  1-abs(sum_key(P*exp(i*gamma*S))); report alongside direct TV. This handles
  phase wrapping and row-global phase shifts. Interpret as descriptive scale,
  not a causal proof. No sweep of dt or gamma is authorized.

## Aggregation and frozen interpretation

Retain per-example arrays and report all stratified cells, so cancellation or
a large CLS-specific effect cannot disappear into a grand mean. Summaries use
unweighted empirical row quantiles; seed summaries weight each seed equally.
Do not mix validation/test sets or sequence lengths into the primary tables.
Any pooled exploratory display is explicitly labelled and secondary.

Descriptive bands frozen before new results:

- A cell is locally small when direct TV mean <0.01 and p95 <0.05. "Uniformly
  small" requires this in every seed/condition/op/layer/head, for both all-row
  and CLS summaries. Otherwise report the exact larger-effect cells, not a
  single blanket label. Values near a boundary are not a statistical discovery.
- A seed/condition/op cell is downstream small when mean abs delta-p(class1)
  <0.01, p95 <0.05, and disagreement <0.01. Loss distribution and margin changes
  are still shown; an accuracy-only null is not enough.
- Uniformly small local and downstream effects support a near-softmax regime
  for these checkpoints. Larger local TV with downstream-small behavior supports
  downstream insensitivity/cancellation as a descriptive explanation. Mixed
  cells are reported as mixed. Larger downstream effects without a consistent
  loss/accuracy benefit support "changes behavior, no observed useful gain".
  None of these conclusions proves representational equivalence or interference.
- A practically noticeable downstream benefit requires mean CE_dt0-CE_normal
  >=0.01 nats and positive benefit in at least 2 of 3 seeds on the same test
  condition/op; also report accuracy differences, with 1 pp as a descriptive
  reference. These are retrospective diagnostic bands, not the original
  continue/stop gate or a license for new training.
